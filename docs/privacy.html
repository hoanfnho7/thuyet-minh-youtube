Privacy Policy

YouTube AI Voice Dubber · Last updated: November 2026

YouTube AI Voice Dubber ("the Extension") is committed to protecting user privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you install and use the Extension.

1. No Personal Data Collection

  • The Extension does not collect, store, sell, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII), browsing history, YouTube account credentials, or usage analytics to any developer-owned servers.
  • All user preferences (target language, voice selection, playback speed mode, volume balance) and optional Bring-Your-Own-Key (BYOK) API keys (OpenAI / Google Cloud) are saved exclusively on your local device via the browser's chrome.storage.local API.

2. Third-Party Services

To provide real-time subtitle translation and AI voice-over, caption segments from the active YouTube video are processed directly from your browser:

  • Subtitle text is sent transiently to translation and Text-to-Speech providers (Google Translate, Microsoft Speech Services, or your configured OpenAI / Google Cloud API endpoints) solely to generate translated captions and audio playback.
  • No intermediary server operated by the developer intercepts, logs, or retains these requests.

3. Browser Permissions Justification

  • storage: Required solely to persist your extension preferences and optional API keys locally on your device.
  • host_permissions (youtube.com and translation/TTS APIs): Required to read video caption tracks on YouTube pages and fetch synthesized speech audio directly from speech providers.
